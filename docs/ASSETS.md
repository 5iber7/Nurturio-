# Assets and licenses

- World illustrations: original procedural Dart drawings in `world_art.dart`. No downloaded image assets.
- Scene animations: original Flame rendering and lightweight transforms.
- Interaction/ambience WAV files: original synthesized sounds generated by `scripts/create-audio.py`; no external samples. Music is optional and disabled by default.
- Nunito: Google Fonts distribution, SIL Open Font License. License is bundled at `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.
- Icons: Flutter Material icons, the original plant notification vector and original Nurturio sprout launcher icons generated by `scripts/create-icons.py`. Store artwork still needs owner review.
- SQLite WASM: sqlite3.dart release artifact version 3.5.2; source/build licenses are supplied by that dependency. Original download: https://github.com/simolus3/sqlite3.dart/releases/tag/sqlite3-3.5.2.

To replace artwork, update the world painter or introduce an asset manifest plus render components; preserve template semantics and accessible labels. Final sprite sheets, mascot poses, store icons, promotional screenshots and curated captioned videos remain production art tasks. Do not invent licenses for replacement art.
