# Privacy policy draft — requires owner review

Nurturio can be played without an account. Learning progress, preferences, the chosen age band and manually saved discoveries are stored on the device. The app does not require a real name, birth date or precise location. It contains no advertising, public profiles or user-to-user chat.

Photos selected for local preview are re-encoded to remove metadata and are not uploaded in the default build. Curated Buzz messages remain in memory on this device. Live AI is unavailable by default. If an eligible AI deployment is enabled in the future, the policy must identify the provider and describe its actual data processing and retention before collecting images or messages.

Optional adult cloud sync uses an email sign-in account and private progress records in Supabase. The app does not currently enable cloud accounts for minors. Parent PIN controls protect local settings but do not establish legal identity or verifiable parental consent.

Readiness notifications are optional. Denying permission does not block play. There are no automatic analytics/crash uploads in the default build.

Local progress can be exported or deleted in the parent area. Cloud account export/deletion require authentication, with recent sign-in required for deletion. Deletion may be delayed by service failures; pending deletion must not be described as successful. Backup/provider retention limits must be disclosed accurately for the chosen deployment.

Before release, fill in the operator's legal name/contact, jurisdiction, hosting regions, retention schedule, support process, consent process if minors use online services, applicable rights and complaint channels. Do not publish this draft as a finalized legal policy.
